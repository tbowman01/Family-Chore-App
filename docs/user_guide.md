## 🚀 Getting Started

### First Time Setup

#### 1. Install the App
- Get the APK from your developer or build from source
- Install on your Android device (iOS coming soon)

#### 2. Create Your Account

**As a Parent (First User):**
1. Open the app
2. Tap **"Create Account"**
3. Enter your details:
   - **Name:** Your full name (e.g., "Sarah Johnson")
   - **Email:** Valid email address
   - **Password:** At least 6 characters
4. Tap **"Create Account"**
5. Select **"Parent"** role
6. Create a new family:
   - Enter your family name (e.g., "Johnson Family")
   - Tap **"Create Family"**
7. **Important:** Save your **Family ID** - you'll need it to add children!

**As a Child:**
1. Open the app
2. Tap **"Create Account"**
3. Enter your details
4. Tap **"Create Account"**
5. Select **"Child"** role
6. Join existing family:
   - Ask your parent for the **Family ID**
   - Enter the Family ID
   - Tap **"Join Family"**

#### 3. Verify Your Setup
- Parents: You should see the parent dashboard
- Children: You should see the child dashboard with no chores yet

## 👨‍👩 Parent Guide

### Dashboard Overview

When you log in as a parent, you'll see:
- **Active Chores:** List of all chores you've created
- **Pending Submissions:** Chores awaiting your review
- **Family Members:** All parents and children in your family
- **Create Chore Button:** Floating action button to add new chores

### Creating a Chore

1. **Tap the "+" button** (bottom right)
2. **Fill in chore details:**

   **Title**
   - Short, clear name (e.g., "Take out trash", "Make bed")

   **Description**
   - What needs to be done
   - Any special instructions
   - Example: "Take both kitchen and bathroom trash to the outside bin"

   **Points**
   - Reward points for completion
   - Suggested scale:
     - 10-20 points: Simple tasks (5 min)
     - 30-50 points: Medium tasks (15-30 min)
     - 60-100 points: Complex tasks (1+ hour)

   **Assign To**
   - Select which child should do this chore
   - Only one child per chore (for now)

   **Due Date**
   - When the chore should be completed
   - Children will see this deadline

3. **Tap "Create Chore"**
4. The chore is now visible to the assigned child!

### Reviewing Submissions

When a child submits a chore:

1. **You'll see it in "Pending Submissions"**
2. **Tap the chore** to open details
3. **Review the photo proof:**
   - Tap the photo to view full size
   - Zoom in if needed
   - Check if the task was completed properly

4. **Make your decision:**

   **If Approved:**
   - Tap **"Approve"**
   - Optionally add feedback: "Great job! The room looks spotless!"
   - Points are automatically awarded to the child

   **If Rejected:**
   - Tap **"Reject"**
   - Add feedback explaining why: "The corners still have dust. Please clean them."
   - Child can resubmit after fixing issues

### Managing Chores

**Edit a Chore:**
1. Long-press on any chore card
2. Select **"Edit"**
3. Make changes
4. Tap **"Save"**

**Delete a Chore:**
1. Long-press on chore card
2. Select **"Delete"**
3. Confirm deletion

**View Chore History:**
- Tap any chore to see:
  - Submission history
  - Approval/rejection log
  - Points awarded

### Managing Family Members

1. **View Members:**
   - Scroll to "Family Members" section
   - See all parents and children

2. **Share Family ID:**
   - Tap **"Family ID"** button
   - Share the ID with family members who need to join

3. **Monitor Points:**
   - View each child's total points
   - Track their progress

### Best Practices for Parents

**Creating Effective Chores:**
- ✅ Use clear, specific titles
- ✅ Include detailed descriptions
- ✅ Set realistic deadlines
- ✅ Assign appropriate point values
- ✅ Take photos of "good examples" to show children

**Reviewing Submissions:**
- ✅ Review submissions promptly (within 24 hours)
- ✅ Provide constructive feedback
- ✅ Be consistent with approval standards
- ✅ Celebrate good work!
- ⚠️ If rejecting, explain clearly what needs improvement

**Point System Tips:**
- 💡 Start with lower points and adjust based on effort
- 💡 Bonus points for exceptional work (add a new chore!)
- 💡 Track points weekly to plan rewards
- 💡 Discuss with children what points can be "traded" for

## 👧👦 Child Guide

### Dashboard Overview

When you log in as a child, you'll see:
- **My Chores:** All chores assigned to you
- **Points:** Your total earned points
- **Filters:** View by Pending, Submitted, or Approved

### Viewing Your Chores

**Chore Card Shows:**
- Title and description
- Points you'll earn
- Due date
- Current status (Pending, Submitted, Approved, Rejected)

**Filter Your Chores:**
1. Tap the filter dropdown at the top
2. Select:
   - **Pending:** Chores you need to complete
   - **Submitted:** Chores awaiting parent review
   - **Approved:** Completed and approved chores
   - **Rejected:** Chores that need to be redone

### Completing a Chore

**Step-by-Step Process:**

1. **Do the chore!**
   - Read the description carefully
   - Complete all tasks listed
   - Do your best work

2. **Take proof photo:**
   - Tap the chore card
   - Tap **"Submit with Photo"**
   - Choose photo source:
     - **Camera:** Take a new photo right now
     - **Gallery:** Choose existing photo

3. **Photo Tips:**
   - 📸 Make sure the area is well-lit
   - 📸 Show the entire completed task
   - 📸 Hold camera steady for clear photo
   - 📸 Include wider view, not just close-up

4. **Submit:**
   - Review your photo
   - Tap **"Submit"**
   - Status changes to "Submitted"

5. **Wait for parent review:**
   - You'll see status update
   - Check back for feedback

### After Parent Reviews

**If Approved ✅**
- Points added to your total!
- Read any feedback from parents
- View your updated points balance
- Great job! 🎉

**If Rejected ❌**
- Don't worry! You can fix it
- Read the feedback carefully
- Redo the chore based on comments
- Take a new photo
- Resubmit

### Tracking Your Points

**View Points:**
- See total at top of dashboard
- Track points earned per chore
- Check your progress

**What Points Mean:**
- Points can be traded for rewards
- Ask your parents what points can buy:
  - Extra screen time?
  - Special treats?
  - Money allowance?
  - Special privileges?

### Best Practices for Children

**Taking Great Photos:**
- ✅ Clean the entire area first
- ✅ Take photo in good lighting
- ✅ Show the whole task (wide angle)
- ✅ Make it look your best!
- ⚠️ Don't take blurry photos
- ⚠️ Don't take photos before completing task

**Getting Approvals:**
- ✅ Complete tasks thoroughly
- ✅ Double-check before taking photo
- ✅ Read descriptions carefully
- ✅ Ask questions if confused
- ✅ Learn from rejected submissions

## 💡 Tips & Best Practices

### For Families

**Setting Up Your System:**
1. **Start small:** Begin with 3-5 simple chores
2. **Build routine:** Assign daily chores consistently
3. **Weekly planning:** Review and plan chores on Sunday
4. **Family meeting:** Discuss the system together
5. **Adjust as needed:** Change points/chores based on what works

**Point System Ideas:**
- 100 points = $1 or 15 min screen time
- 500 points = Special treat or outing
- 1000 points = Bigger reward (toy, game, etc.)
- Bonus multipliers for streak days

**Communication:**
- Parents: Provide specific, kind feedback
- Children: Ask questions before starting
- Everyone: Be patient as you learn the system

### Photo Proof Tips

**Good Photo Examples:**
- Clean room: Show full room from doorway
- Dishes: Show empty sink and clean counters
- Trash: Show empty bins with new bags
- Vacuuming: Show vacuum lines in carpet

**Bad Photo Examples:**
- ❌ Blurry or dark photos
- ❌ Close-up of one small area
- ❌ Photo taken at wrong angle
- ❌ Photo that doesn't show the completed task

## ❓ FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Can one chore be assigned to multiple children?**
A: Not in the alpha version. Each chore can only be assigned to one child. Create separate chores if multiple children should do the same task.

**Q: Can I have multiple families?**
A: Not yet. Each user can only belong to one family in the alpha version.

**Q: How do I remove a family member?**
A: This feature isn't available yet. Contact your developer for database changes.

**Q: Can children create their own chores?**
A: No. Only parents can create, edit, and delete chores.

### Parent Questions

**Q: Can I change a child's points?**
A: Not directly. Points are only awarded through chore approvals. To add bonus points, create a special "Bonus" chore.

**Q: Can I schedule recurring chores?**
A: Not in the alpha version. You'll need to manually create chores each time. This is planned for the beta release!

**Q: What if a child submits the wrong photo?**
A: Reject the submission with feedback. The child can resubmit with a new photo.

**Q: Can I see photo history?**
A: You can see the most recent submission photo. Full history isn't available in the alpha version.

### Child Questions

**Q: Can I do chores not assigned to me?**
A: No. You can only complete and submit chores assigned to you by parents.

**Q: What if I can't take a photo right now?**
A: Complete the chore and take the photo when you can. Just make sure the area stays clean until you photograph it!

**Q: Can I delete my submitted photo and resubmit?**
A: Once submitted, you must wait for parent review. If rejected, you can then resubmit.

**Q: What if my sibling does my chore?**
A: That's between you and your sibling! The person whose account submits the photo gets the points.

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Login Issues

**Problem: "Invalid email or password"**
- ✅ Check your email is spelled correctly
- ✅ Check caps lock is off
- ✅ Try password reset if forgotten

**Problem: "Network error"**
- ✅ Check your internet connection
- ✅ Try switching between WiFi and mobile data
- ✅ Restart the app

### Chore Issues

**Problem: Chores not showing up**
- ✅ Pull down to refresh the screen
- ✅ Check you're using the correct filter
- ✅ Verify you're logged in as the right user

**Problem: Can't submit photo**
- ✅ Check app has camera permissions
- ✅ Check app has storage permissions
- ✅ Ensure photo is under 5MB
- ✅ Try taking a new photo instead of gallery

**Problem: Submission stuck on "Uploading"**
- ✅ Check internet connection
- ✅ Wait a few minutes (large photos take time)
- ✅ If still stuck after 5 min, restart app

### Points Issues

**Problem: Points not updating**
- ✅ Pull down to refresh
- ✅ Check if parent has approved (not just submitted)
- ✅ Verify in "Approved" filter

**Problem: Wrong points awarded**
- ✅ Ask parent - they may have edited the chore
- ✅ Check the chore details for current point value

### App Issues

**Problem: App crashes**
- ✅ Restart the app
- ✅ Clear app cache (Settings > Apps > Family Chores > Clear Cache)
- ✅ Update to latest version

**Problem: Slow performance**
- ✅ Close other apps
- ✅ Restart your device
- ✅ Check available storage space

### Still Having Problems?

1. Try `make troubleshoot` if you're a developer
2. Check the [Quick Start Guide](quick_start.md#troubleshooting)
3. Report issues to your family's app administrator
4. For developers: Check logs with `adb logcat | grep flutter`
